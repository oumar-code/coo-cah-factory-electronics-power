# Implementation Plan

**Factory:** Coo-Cah Garage & Power Electronics Factory, Sagamu, Ogun State  
**Repository:** `coo-cah-factory-electronics-power`  
**Document Ref:** CCG-PE-IMPL-001 | **Version:** 1.0

---

This page mirrors the factory implementation plan for MkDocs navigation and operational reference.

## 1. Delivery Objectives

- Commission Phase 1 manufacturing capability for inverters, SCCs, UPS, power strips, and power tools.
- Ensure MES-first traceability with test gating and auditable serial records.
- Complete SON/NCC/NESREA readiness for priority SKUs and site operations.
- Protect intra-group supply commitments while ramping commercial output.
- Operationalise controlled AI capabilities for quality and maintenance support.

## 2. Workstreams

| Workstream | Focus |
|---|---|
| Manufacturing industrialisation | SMT, winding, assembly, and load-bank readiness |
| MES/DT integration | End-to-end serial, test, and telemetry flow integrity |
| Compliance execution | SON type-test, NCC type approval, NESREA obligations |
| Supply coordination | Internal allocation rules, protected stock, escalation paths |
| AI platform rollout | Assistive use cases now, predictive controls after validation |

## 3. Milestone Gates

| Gate | Exit Condition |
|---|---|
| Site and utilities ready | Power, ESD, safety, and production utilities commissioned |
| Equipment SAT complete | Core lines pass readiness and capability checks |
| MES production go-live | Full traceability and test-record linkage active |
| Pilot lot validation | Quality/reliability thresholds achieved on priority SKUs |
| Internal supply launch | Stable deliveries to sister factories begin |
| Commercial release | Internal demand protection maintained and quality remains stable |

## 4. Governance

| Cadence | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Daily control | Throughput, defects, downtime actioning |
| Weekly program review | Risks, blockers, milestone health |
| Monthly compliance/AI review | Certification and AI control-state decisions |
| Quarterly steering | Phase-gate and escalation decisions |

## 5. Key Risks

- Semiconductor allocation volatility
- Certification timeline slippage (SON/NCC)
- MES/test integration drift
- Commissioning delays from power instability
- Internal-demand spikes affecting availability

## 6. Success Conditions

1. Priority SKUs meet quality and test-performance targets.
2. MES and DT records remain complete, consistent, and auditable.
3. Compliance pathways remain evidence-complete and on schedule.
4. Intra-group deliveries meet protected service levels.
5. AI outputs remain governed, measurable, and safely deployed.
